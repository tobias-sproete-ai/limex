# Scope and nonclaims

## In scope

- closed-schema structural validation of an accreditation packet;
- two routes: institutional and independent researcher;
- exact two-reviewer separation, scope binding and conflict declaration;
- binding to one pseudonymous subject, project, declared research purpose, credential key, short validity window, finite scopes and finite budget;
- fresh revocation snapshot requirement;
- deterministic deny/hold decisions and lifecycle projection;
- no founder-recognized key, privileged role, reopening path or same-identity mutation mechanism;
- local no-network tests and self-excluding package identity.

## Not claimed

- no proof that a person is a scientist, honest, competent or harmless;
- no biometric, document, university, eduGAIN, ORCID, WebAuthn or DPoP verifier;
- no certificate-path, assertion-signature or live revocation verification;
- no token/capability issuer, gateway, model endpoint, sponsor key or compute dispatch;
- no prevention of collusion, coercion, a compromised trusted issuer, or downstream misuse of legitimately obtained outputs;
- no technical purpose restriction over publicly downloaded artefacts;
- no claim that repository administration prevents deletion, replacement attempts or successor publication; cryptographic identity only makes such changes distinguishable from the sealed release;
- no legal, privacy, export-control, ethics, sanctions or public-release approval;
- no Goldbach proof or research-payload release.

`PASS` in this package means local conformance to the declared structural scope. It is not person accreditation and not service authorization.
