## Access for Scientific Inquiry

Knowledge advances when it can be examined, challenged, and extended. LIMEX Science is intended to be made available free of charge to designated users in the academic and scientific community after the applicable release and individual-access gates have been satisfied.

LIMEX Science is not open source. Its purpose is to make a rigorous scientific environment available for defined research use while preserving a controlled technical foundation.

## A Foundation for Verifiable Discovery

Modern research increasingly confronts problems whose complexity exceeds the limits of intuition and conventional heuristic search. LIMEX Science combines deterministic formal methods with modern artificial intelligence to support scientific inquiry that is more structured, traceable, and verifiable.

Its purpose is not to replace scientific judgment. It is designed to help researchers turn generated ideas into hypotheses whose assumptions, reasoning steps, and results can be examined rigorously.

LIMEX Science is intended to support a broad range of research workflows:

- **Model-Agnostic AI Collaboration**  
  The architecture is designed to work across different foundation models and to translate generative output into hypotheses with traceable assumptions and inspectable reasoning steps.

- **Complex Time-Series Analysis**  
  The architecture can integrate dedicated time-series models such as TimesFM to support the analysis of high-dimensional, nonlinear temporal data, including pattern recognition, anomaly detection, and forecasting.

- **Cross-Disciplinary Research**  
  Its formal reduction and verification methods may support suitable workflows in fields such as computational medicine, molecular structural biology, high-energy physics, materials engineering, and pure mathematics. Applicability and evidentiary strength remain specific to each domain, model, and dataset.

- **Verification by Design**  
  Artificial intelligence can generate useful starting points, but generated output must not be treated as scientific evidence without scrutiny. LIMEX Science is built on the principle that important conclusions should remain inspectable and reproducible—and formally verifiable whenever a valid formalization and suitable evidence make that possible.

Subject to the applicable release and access gates, we intend to offer this infrastructure to designated laboratories, universities, and independent researchers working on difficult questions. May it help make scientific work more transparent, more rigorous, and more capable of producing results that explain, improve, and expand our understanding of the world.
